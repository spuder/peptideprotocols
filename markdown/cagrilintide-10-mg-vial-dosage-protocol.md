## Dosing & Reconstitution Guide

### Phase 2 4.5 mg Target Arm
*Phase 2 4.5 mg Target Arm*

| Week/Phase | Dose | Units (mL) |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | 0.6 mg | 18 units |
| Weeks 3–4 | 1.2 mg | 36 units |
| Weeks 5–6 | 2.4 mg | 72 units |
| Week 7 onward in the 26-week trial | 4.5 mg | 135 units |

### Reconstitution Steps
1. Prepare: Verify the 10 mg vial, compatible sterile diluent, alcohol swabs, and sterile syringes; clean the vial stopper.
2. Add: Introduce diluent slowly down the vial wall until the final volume is 3.0 mL .
3. Dissolve: Gently swirl or roll the vial until the solution is clear; do not shake.
4. Label and store: Mark ~3.33 mg/mL and the preparation date, then refrigerate at 2–8 °C (35.6–46.4 °F) protected from light.
