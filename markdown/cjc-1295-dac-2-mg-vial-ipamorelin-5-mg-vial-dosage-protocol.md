## Dosing & Reconstitution Guide

### CJC-1295 DAC (2 mg Vial) — Weekly Subcutaneous
*CJC-1295 DAC (2 mg Vial) — Weekly Subcutaneous*

| Week/Phase | Dose | Units (mL) |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 1000 mcg (1.0 mg) | 100 units (1.0 mL) |
| Aggressive | 2000 mcg (2.0 mg) | 100 units × 2 injections (2.0 mL total) |

### Ipamorelin (5 mg Vial) — Daily Subcutaneous
*Ipamorelin (5 mg Vial) — Daily Subcutaneous*

| Week/Phase | Dose | Units (mL) |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 (Initiation) | 100 mcg | 6 units (0.06 mL) |
| Weeks 3–4 (Titration) | 150 mcg | 9 units (0.09 mL) |
| Weeks 5+ (Maintenance) | 200 mcg | 12 units (0.12 mL) |
| Aggressive (Optional) | 300 mcg | 18 units (0.18 mL) |

### Combined Stack Schedule — Standard Approach
*Combined Stack Schedule — Standard Approach*

| Week/Phase | Dose | Units (mL) |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | 1000 mcg (100 units) once weekly | 100 mcg (6 units) once daily |
| Weeks 3–4 | 1000 mcg (100 units) once weekly | 150 mcg (9 units) once daily |
| Weeks 5–12 | 1000 mcg (100 units) once weekly | 200 mcg (12 units) once daily |

### Reconstitution Steps
1. Draw 2.0 mL bacteriostatic water with a sterile syringe.
2. Inject slowly down the vial wall; avoid foaming or direct stream onto powder.
3. Gently swirl/roll until fully dissolved (do not shake).
4. Label with date and refrigerate at 2–8 °C (35.6–46.4 °F) .
