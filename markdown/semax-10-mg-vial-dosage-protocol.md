| 10.0 mL | About 7.0 mL | 1,000 mcg/mL |

### Reconstitution Steps
1. Confirm the 10 mg vial, sterile research vehicle, 5 mL or 10 mL actuator bottle, and documented pump output.
2. Add 3.0 mL of compatible sterile vehicle to the vial, then gently swirl until dissolved. Do not shake.
3. Transfer the complete solution to the actuator bottle and add enough vehicle to reach exactly 5.0 mL or 10.0 mL .
4. Secure and label the bottle, prime the pump as directed, then refrigerate at 4°C and protect from light; the supplier lists up to four weeks after reconstitution [8] .
