# CJC-1295 + GHRP-2 (10 mg Blend) Dosage Protocol ()

## Dosing & Reconstitution Guide

### Standard / Gradual Approach
*Standard / Gradual Approach (3 mL = ~3.33 mg/mL)*

| Week/Phase | Dose | Units (mL) |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | 150 mcg (0.15 mg) | 4.5 units (0.045 mL) |
| Weeks 3–12 | 300 mcg (0.30 mg) | 9 units (0.09 mL) |

**Frequency: Tie timing to the research design. Human work supports acute synergistic GH release when GHRH and a growth hormone-releasing peptide are combined [6] . The cited study used controlled intravenous administration and does not define a bedtime subcutaneous schedule for this exact blend.**

### Advanced / Twice-Daily Approach
*Advanced / Twice-Daily Approach (3 mL = ~3.33 mg/mL)*

| Week/Phase | Dose | Units (mL) |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | 100 mcg (0.10 mg) | 3 units (0.03 mL) |
| Weeks 3–12 | 150 mcg (0.15 mg) | 4.5 units (0.045 mL) |

### Reconstitution Steps
1. Draw 3.0 mL bacteriostatic water with a sterile syringe.
2. Inject slowly down the vial wall; avoid foaming.
3. Gently swirl/roll until dissolved (do not shake).
4. Label and refrigerate at 2–8 °C (35.6–46.4 °F) , protected from light.
