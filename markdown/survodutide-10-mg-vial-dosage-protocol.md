# Survodutide (10 mg Vial)

- Scraped: 2026-08-13T17:49:43+00:00
- Reconstitution: 2.0 mL -> 5.0 mg/mL

## Dosing & Reconstitution Guide

### Standard / Gradual Titration
*Standard / Gradual Titration (2 mL = 5 mg/mL)*

| Week/Phase | Dose | Units (mL) |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | 0.6 mg | 600 mcg |
| Weeks 3–4 | 1.2 mg | 1200 mcg |
| Weeks 5–6 | 1.8 mg | 1800 mcg |
| Weeks 7–8 | 2.4 mg | 2400 mcg |
| Weeks 9–10 | 3.6 mg | 3600 mcg |
| Weeks 11–12 | 4.8 mg | 4800 mcg |
| Week 13+ | 6.0 mg | 6000 mcg |

### Reconstitution Steps
1. Draw 2.0 mL bacteriostatic water with a sterile syringe.
2. Inject slowly down the vial wall; avoid foaming.
3. Gently swirl/roll until dissolved (do not shake).
4. Label and refrigerate at 2–8 °C (35.6–46.4 °F) , protected from light.
