## Supplies Needed

- 12 weeks at ~300 mcg/day avg ≈: **5–6 vials**

- 3 vials: **9 mL (also: 1 bottle required)**
- 5 vials: **15 mL (also: 2 bottles required)**
- 6 vials: **18 mL (also: 2 bottles required)**
