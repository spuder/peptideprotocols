## Dosing & Reconstitution Guide

### TB-500 Protocol
*TB-500 Protocol (2 mL Reconstitution = 2.5 mg/mL)*

| Week/Phase | Dose | Units (mL) |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–4 | 1,250 mcg (1.25 mg) | 50 units (0.50 mL) |
| Weeks 5–12 | 1,250 mcg (1.25 mg) | 50 units (0.50 mL) |

### BPC-157 Protocol
*BPC-157 Protocol (2 mL Reconstitution = 2.5 mg/mL)*

| Week/Phase | Dose | Units (mL) |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | 250 mcg (0.25 mg) | 10 units (0.10 mL) |
| Weeks 3–8 | 500 mcg (0.50 mg) | 20 units (0.20 mL) |
| Weeks 9–12 | 250 mcg (0.25 mg) | 10 units (0.10 mL) |

### Reconstitution Steps
1. Draw 2.0 mL bacteriostatic water with a sterile syringe.
2. Inject slowly down the vial wall; avoid foaming.
3. Gently swirl/roll until dissolved (do not shake).
4. Label and refrigerate at 2–8 °C (35.6–46.4 °F) , protected from light.
